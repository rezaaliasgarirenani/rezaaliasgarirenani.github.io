Electronics & applied physics.

> Background

I'm a researcher in electronics and applied physics. My background at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) spans aerospace engineering, semiconductor devices, and plasma systems.

I work across the full experiment: designing digital logic, building verification tools, automating instruments, and interpreting measurements. I enjoy the point where a mathematical model meets a physical device.
