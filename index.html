Research

RTL & Verification

  • Developed Verilog RTL, a fixed-point arithmetic library, and LUT-based function approximations using Horner’s method.
  • Implemented rgb2hsv, Sobel operators, tone mapping, demosaicing, lens distortion correction, and resizing in manual and Simulink-generated HDL.
  • Built AXI and AXI4-Stream SystemVerilog testbenches; compared reconstructed frames with Python, MATLAB, and Simulink reference models; automated simulation and output analysis with Python and cocotb.
  • Synthesized and implemented designs in Vivado; verified ISP pipelines on Xilinx Zybo Z7-20 and Nexys Video boards.

Semiconductor Physics

  • Characterized aluminum-gated SiO2 MOS capacitors after 1–20 keV electron and 1 MeV gamma irradiation using capacitance–voltage (C–V) and thermally stimulated current (TSC) measurements over 80–400 K.
  • Combined C–V shifts and TSC spectra to locate electrically active traps, classify electron- or hole-trapping behavior, estimate activation energies, and calculate interface-state density.
  • Developed MATLAB applications to automate TSC, C–V, current–voltage, current–time, and deep-level transient spectroscopy measurements.

Plasma Systems

  • Conducted FPGA-board irradiation experiments using 25–60 keV electron beams at currents up to 100 mA.
  • Used low-pressure oxygen atmospheres (10−6–50 Torr), generating plasma and X-rays.
  • Applied combined thermal cycling (218–393 K) and surface charging to evaluate FPGA reliability under plasma conditions.
