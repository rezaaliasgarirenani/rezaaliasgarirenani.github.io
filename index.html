01 / What I investigate

Research, across scales.

From the logic inside a chip to the environments around it. My work connects digital design with experimental physics.

01 / DIGITAL DESIGN

Algorithms into hardware.

Fixed-point DSP and FPGA image processing, from manual Verilog and Simulink-generated HDL to simulation, synthesis, and board-level verification.

Verilog / SystemVerilogFPGAImage processing
Methods & tools

AXI and AXI4-Stream testbenches; Python and cocotb verification; Vivado synthesis. Pipelines include demosaicing, Sobel operators, rgb2hsv, tone mapping, distortion correction, and resizing.

02 / DEVICE PHYSICS

Understanding radiation effects.

Electrical characterization of irradiated MOS structures. I use capacitance–voltage and thermally stimulated current measurements to investigate electrically active defects.

MOS devicesC–V / TSCMATLAB automation
Methods & tools

Low-energy electron and gamma irradiation; measurements over 80–400 K; trap analysis and interface-state characterization. Instrument automation with Keithley, Keysight, Lakeshore, and Zurich Instruments equipment.

03 / PLASMA & SPACE

Testing beyond the lab bench.

Electron-beam plasma facilities for simulating space-flight conditions, investigating FPGA reliability under irradiation, thermal cycling, and surface charging.

Electron-beam plasmaRadiation testingSpace environments
Experimental conditions

Electron beams of 25–60 keV, up to 100 mA, in low-pressure oxygen atmospheres. Combined thermal cycling over 218–393 K and surface charging to study FPGA behavior.
